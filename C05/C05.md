# Challenge 05

## Title
The Signal Between Worlds

## Difficulty
Easy

## Points
100

## Description
During the Battle of New York, S.H.I.E.L.D. intercepted a strange audio transmission originating from Stark Tower's roof array. It was flagged as non-standard — not speech, not noise. Analysts ran it through every audio tool they had. Nothing readable. Vision believes the signal was designed by someone who thinks differently about what "up" means. Maybe you do too.

## Files
- `avengers_comms.wav`

## Hint
*"The mind is not a vessel to be filled, but a fire to be kindled."* — What you see depends on how you look at it.

## Solution
Open `avengers_comms.wav` in **Audacity** or **Sonic Visualizer** and switch to **Spectrogram view**.

The flag is embedded in the spectrogram but **upside down** — the image must be flipped vertically to read correctly.

In Audacity:
1. Click the track dropdown → Spectrogram
2. The text appears mirrored on the frequency axis
3. Flip the image vertically (screenshot → flip) to read the flag

The trap: every tool defaults to low-frequency at the bottom. The flag was encoded with high-frequency at the bottom — flipping the render reveals the text.
