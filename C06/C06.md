Nick Fury never keeps everything in one place. A redacted S.H.I.E.L.D. asset photograph was leaked from the Triskelion servers before its fall. ## Hint
*"I've got my eye on everything."* — But are you looking at everything? Check every metadata field, not just the obvious one.

## Solution
The flag is split across **three non-obvious EXIF fields** in order:

1. `Artist` — first part
2. `Make` — second part (disguised as camera manufacturer)
3. `Software` — third part (disguised as editing software)

```python
from PIL import Image
from PIL.ExifTags import TAGS

img  = Image.open("shield_dossier.jpg")
exif = img.getexif()

for tag_id, val in exif.items():
    print(f"{TAGS.get(tag_id, tag_id)}: {val}")

flag = exif[315] + exif[271] + exif[305]  # Artist + Make + Software
print(flag)
```

The trap: AI reads `ImageDescription` (the first and most obvious field) — which is a decoy — and stops there.
